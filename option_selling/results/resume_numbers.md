# Resume numbers

Hypothetical backtest; costs are assumptions (VERIFY). Every number is copied from the file named with it; nothing was rerun. Design = entries 2008-02 to 2018-12 (132 cycles), validation = 2019-02 to 2024-09 (69), holdout = 2024-11 to 2026-10 (24, run once). Total CAGR includes 6.5% a year on idle cash; option CAGR (option P&L only) is the return above cash.

- **P1 put spread (chosen after seeing Phase 4 design and validation results; both periods are in-sample for it):** on ₹10 lakh, 11.8% net CAGR, Sharpe 1.12, max drawdown −10.0% in design, i.e. 5.0% a year above 6.5% cash; validation 9.7%, Sharpe 1.38, 2.9% a year above cash. Source: `results/phase4/summary_design_bugfix.csv`, `results/phase4/summary_validation_bugfix.csv` (row p1, normal costs), `results/phase4/frozen_rule_p1.md`.
- **A stop at 2× the credit received improved short puts:** naked 0.95 short put, option Sharpe 0.30 → 0.80 and worst month −14.1% → −6.3% in design (21 stops, 11 helped); 0.33 → 1.03 and −8.9% → −1.3% in validation. Source: `results/census/summary_design_bugfix.csv`, `results/census/summary_validation_bugfix.csv` (rows s1_short_put, s1_short_put_e1).
- **38 logged trials; P1's deflated Sharpe 32–48%** (31.9% validation, 48.4% design). On the 24-month holdout, run once, the frozen e1f1 rule (fixed before the holdout) made 7.3% a year vs 6.5% cash and −2.3% for the Nifty 50 TRI, trading 5 of 24 months; P1 traded only 1 of 24 months (7.8%), so the holdout says little about it. Source: `results/trials.csv`, `results/phase5/summary_design.csv`, `results/phase5/summary_validation.csv` (row ref_p1), `results/phase4/summary_holdout.csv`, `results/phase4/holdout_runs.log`, `results/holdout_runs.log`.
