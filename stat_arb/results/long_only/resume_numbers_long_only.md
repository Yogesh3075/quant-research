# Resume numbers: long-only pairs variants

Source: `results/long_only/summary_long_only.md`, generated by `long_only_pairs.py`. Development is 2011-2022. "Seen holdout" is 2023 to Nov 2026, which the long-short rebuild had already looked at, so it is not out of sample.

- Split 610 long-short pair trades into legs. Against the equal-weight Nifty 500, the long leg added 0.29% a trade (t = 0.59) and the short leg 0.98% (t = 2.13). The relative-value signal sat in the short leg, not the long leg.
- Tested long-only pair switching on the point-in-time Nifty 500, 20 pairs per 6-month window. It beat a 50/50 buy-and-hold of the same pairs by 0.12% a month in development (t = 0.88) and by −0.02% a month in the seen holdout (t = −0.13). Deflated Sharpe over 9 logged trials: 0.26.
- Hedged the cheap leg with Nifty 50 futures. Sharpe was 0.10 in development and −0.12 in the seen holdout, so there is no edge after costs.
