# Resume numbers: pairs trading on the point-in-time Nifty 500

Every number is from `results/summary.md` (generated by `pairs_nifty500.py`) or from `results/pairs_per_window.csv` and `results/trial_log.csv`.

- Built a pairs-trading backtest on the point-in-time Nifty 500, 2011 to Nov 2026, with no look-ahead. It tested 145,841 intra-sector candidate pairs (Engle-Granger, half-life filter) across 32 rolling 12-month formation windows, traded 640 selected pairs, and charged costs on both legs.
- Development, 2011-2022: Sharpe 0.25 (deflated Sharpe 0.18 over 6 logged trials). **Locked holdout, 2023 to Nov 2026, run once: Sharpe −0.54, CAGR −3.3%, max drawdown −16.4%.**
- Built point-in-time F&O eligibility from 32 NSE F&O bhavcopies. The shortable F&O subset had a holdout Sharpe of −0.17. Concluded there is no edge after costs.
