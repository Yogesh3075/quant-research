# Resume numbers

All numbers come from `results/summary.md` (generated by `report.py`). Development is 2015-2022, walk-forward. "Seen" is 2023 to Sep 2026: momentum results for that period had already been looked at, so it is not out of sample.

- Built a walk-forward LightGBM vs linear-blend test on the point-in-time Nifty 500 (12 features, 62k stock-months, 3 pre-specified trials, no tuning). LightGBM's development IC was 0.052 (t = 3.60) against 0.064 for the linear blend. Net of costs, LightGBM minus the linear blend was −0.06% a month (t = −0.23) in development and +0.28% (t = 0.59) in the seen period.
- After costs, the LightGBM top-30 portfolio beat equal weight by only 0.25% a month in development (t = 0.84) and by −0.07% a month in the seen period. Turnover was 7.5x a year and cost drag 3.0% a year. Its alpha against the IIMA four factors was 1.4% a year (t = 0.56).
- SHAP showed the model mostly rediscovered momentum (43% of mean |SHAP|) and low volatility/low beta (38%). Plain momentum beat both models over equal weight: 0.67% a month in development (t = 2.08).
